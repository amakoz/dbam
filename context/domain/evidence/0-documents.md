## Product goals

- `context/foundation/prd.md:18` — "In Poland, adults 30+ forget or postpone routine, age-appropriate exams and screenings — no system prompts them at the point when they become eligible, so exams silently lapse and a treatable condition can go undetected until it's more serious."
- `context/foundation/prd.md:24` — persona: "an adult aged 30+ living in Poland, with a busy daily routine and no strict health-tracking habit, who tends to let non-urgent medical appointments (age-eligible screenings, checkups, or a booked procedure) slip without an external prompt."
- `context/foundation/prd.md:32` (Primary) — "The end-to-end v1 flow works: a user signs up, completes onboarding, receives personalized screening recommendations from their profile data, can mark an exam as already handled (which schedules the next due date by its repeat interval), and receives an opt-in reminder when a screening becomes due."
- `context/foundation/prd.md:36` (Secondary) — "Users act on a reminder — i.e. book or attend the suggested exam, not just see the recommendation."
- `context/foundation/prd.md:40-41` (Guardrails) — "Recommendations never state or imply a diagnosis …" / "A user's health/profile data is never exposed to another user."
- `context/foundation/roadmap.md:40` (vision recap) — "Dbam turns a short profile into an importance-labeled list of due screenings and keeps reminding the user from recommendation → appointment date → confirmed completion → next due date."
- Non-goals `context/foundation/prd.md:160-163`: no NFZ queue-watcher, no medicine tracker, no in-app booking ("the app only records a date the user enters"), no shared/family accounts.
